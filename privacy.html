Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

AITG ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010 ("PDPA"). This policy explains how we handle the information you provide when registering for The Agentic AI Leap event.

1. What we collect

When you register for the event, we collect: your full name, work email address, mobile number, company name, job title, and your stated area of interest. We also collect basic technical logs (IP address, browser type) for security purposes.

2. Why we collect it

We use your data to: (a) process and confirm your event registration; (b) contact you with event updates, logistics and reminders; (c) manage seating, catering and event safety; (d) follow up with event materials or related business information if you opt in; (e) meet legal or regulatory obligations.

3. Who we share it with

Your registration details are shared only with our event co-organiser, Akamai Technologies, and vendors strictly necessary for running the event (e.g. the venue, registration-system provider). We never sell your personal data.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on secured servers operated by AITG and its service providers, protected by access controls, encryption in transit and audit logging. We retain registration data for up to 24 months after the event for business-follow-up purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymised, unless a longer retention is required by law.

5. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may request access to, correction of, or withdrawal of consent for the processing of your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at agentic@aitg.com.my. Withdrawing consent may affect our ability to administer your event attendance.

6. Photography at the event

The event may be photographed and recorded for documentation and marketing. If you do not wish to appear in published materials, please inform the registration desk on arrival.

7. Third-party links and widgets

This site embeds an AI chat assistant and links to partner websites. Those services operate under their own privacy policies; we encourage you to review them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

Data protection enquiries: agentic@aitg.com.my · AITG, Penang, Malaysia.
